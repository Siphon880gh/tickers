Before you trade: a simple research checklist
  1. Know what you own. A stock is a business. A bullion trust and a futures fund are not.
  2. Check the business or fund. Revenue and debt for a company. Holdings, fees, and the prospectus for a fund.
  3. Look for the next catalyst. Earnings, inflation reports, inventories, and supply changes. Separate an announced event from a rumor.
  4. Define your downside. Decide how much loss you can tolerate before choosing a position size.
