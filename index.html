Gold

Research the metal itself or the businesses that mine it. A miner is not the same bet as a bullion trust.

  • GLDSPDR Gold SharesA trust holding physical gold.
  • IAUiShares Gold TrustAnother physically backed gold trust. Compare fees and trading costs.
  • GDXVanEck Gold Miners ETFA basket of gold-mining companies.
  • NEMNewmontA gold-producing business. Costs and output matter, not just the metal.
  • BBarrick MiningA gold and copper producer. Its former NYSE ticker was GOLD.
