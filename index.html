Before you trade: a simple research checklist
  1. A stock is a business. A stock ETF holds a basket of businesses. Bullion trusts and futures funds have different structures and risks.
  2. For a company, read revenue, profit, debt, cash flow, and management guidance. For a fund, check its issuer, , , index, , and prospectus.
  3. Earnings, , , , and supply changes can matter. Separate an announced event from speculation.
  4. Check the market price. , trading volume, , and help you understand what a trade may cost.
  5. Social sentiment reflects the people posting, not every investor. Compare claims with filings and primary sources.
  6. Decide how much loss you can tolerate before choosing a position size. and any tax consequences.
